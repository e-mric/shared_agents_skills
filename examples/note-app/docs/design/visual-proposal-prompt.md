Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: polished desktop note app design proposal, landscape image.
Primary request: Design an exceptionally thoughtful, minimal but visually rich note app named "marginal". This is the finished app revealed through an interactive workflow presentation. Show one complete, legible, realistic desktop interface, straight-on, filling the image, no device frame or perspective. Balance illustration with genuinely useful writing space.
Composition: Slim left navigation approximately 13% width; a visual notes library approximately 42%; a selected note editor approximately 45%. Clean alignment, generous spacing, refined typographic hierarchy. Alice blue canvas, white paper surfaces, subtle rules, restrained rounded corners, almost no shadows. Serif editorial headings paired with clean humanist sans-serif controls.
Palette: Deep Space Blue #13293D text, Baltic Blue #006494 primary buttons, Cerulean #247BA0 ink illustrations, Blue Bell #1B98E0 selected accents, Alice Blue #E8F1F2 background, white surfaces. Artwork stays in this palette with subtle pale blue washes and navy fine linework.
Illustration direction: bespoke editorial ink and cut-paper illustrations, poetic but concrete, with fine botanical detail and tactile paper grain. Substantial visual covers that help identify notes: a paper boat on undulating blue water; a winding path through blue hills and tall plants; a collection of folded paper ideas and stars. Sophisticated independent design journal quality. Do not reduce these to tiny icons.
Left navigation: small "marginal" wordmark, understated pen nib mark, "All notes" selected, "Favorites", "Archive", bottom caption "Stored in this browser". No account, avatar, AI button, sync or fake metrics.
Library: heading "A little room for thought." with small line "Your notes, at your own pace." A simple search field and one Baltic blue "+ New note" button. Below a curated two-column grid of four spacious note cards with distinct cover illustrations in the top half and readable titles and short excerpts below. Selected first card titled "A thought worth keeping", other cards "Weekend wanderings", "Ideas for later", "Small observations". Dates unobtrusive. First card has thin blue selection outline. Mix illustrated covers and a typographic paper preview to give gentle rhythm.
Editor: wide white surface, quiet top toolbar "All notes / A thought worth keeping" and modest "Saved" status. A broad panoramic illustration of the paper boat sailing between gently rolling blue hills and reeds at top, around one fifth of editor height. Beneath, big elegant navy serif title "A thought worth keeping". Body text with comfortable line height: "Good ideas rarely arrive fully formed." blank line then "Give them a little space. Write one sentence, follow the next, and keep what matters." Lower down a small divider and another paragraph "Build something small. Make it useful. Return to it with fresh eyes." Keep most lower editor area empty for writing. No rich text toolbar, no decorative widgets.
Constraints: This is a design proposal screenshot, not a marketing landing page or moodboard. No outer heading, no explanatory annotations, no presentation navigation overlaid. Illustration must be prominent in note covers but never behind body text. Readable typography and realistic controls. Minimalism, warmth, visual memory and calm. Avoid purple gradients, dashboard analytics, generic SaaS cards, glassmorphism, stock photography and excessive ornament.
